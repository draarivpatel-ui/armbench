This is ArmBench, an image and video workload benchmark built for the OpenCV AI Competition on Arm. Open the workload bench, choose an image size and iteration count, and run real measurements in your browser. Resize, grayscale, blur, edge detection, histogram, and a full video pipeline, every number timed on this machine's Arm CPU with the seed and settings shown beside it. The baseline compare panel puts those measured results next to estimated cloud figures, and the distinction is engineered to be unmistakable. Measured values are solid, estimated values are hatched and dashed, and every estimate carries its assumptions in plain sight. The environment card states it plainly. These numbers were measured on Apple Silicon Arm locally, not on AWS Graviton, and the report export generates the exact methodology and commands a judge needs to reproduce the run. The node script in the repo runs the same suite from the command line. Honest measurements first, estimates labeled, reproducibility throughout.
